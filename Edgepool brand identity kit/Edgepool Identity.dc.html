Brand kit · v1.0 · September 2026

Automated Solana vaults powered by performance-ranked traders.

Edgepool connects investor capital with skilled on-chain traders through transparent automated vaults. The identity is built to feel calm, precise and accountable: a single signal colour, a constructed mark, and a type system that treats numbers as the main content.

01 · Exploration

Three territories, one synthesis

Quick sketches to test each idea for distinctiveness, scalability to 16px and relevance to the product.

Territory 1
Converging Capital

Controlled streams merge into one pool. Strong product story; loses its shape below 32px and drifts toward a generic "network" icon.

Territory 2
Follow the Edge

A leading route with a signal dot, followed by secondary paths. The dot is the most ownable element; the paths alone read as a chart.

Territory 3
E + Pool

Nested rounded contours form an E and a basin. Reads as a letter first and scales well once the contours reduce to one.

Selected · 3 + 2
The Edge E

Territory 3's single contour gives a legible E and a pool-shaped basin. Territory 2's signal dot sits ahead of the middle arm: the selected trader the vault follows.

03 · Logo system

Lockups and colourways

Use the horizontal lockup by default. Stacked is for square and centred formats; the symbol alone is for app, avatar and in-product use once the name is already present.

04 · Wordmark

Lowercase, drawn to the mark's geometry

The wordmark is custom lettering, not typed. Every bowl is the symbol's rounded contour, so "pool" becomes two identical basins and the "e" repeats the E's arm structure. It is monoline at 21% of x-height.

Recommendededgepool

Reads as a product, not an institution. The repeated bowls give the name a recognisable rhythm and pair with the symbol's weight.

ExploredEdgepool
Edgepool

The capital E competes with the symbol and the lockup reads as "E E-dgepool". Keep title case for running text only: "Edgepool vaults".

09 · Supporting language

Routes, basins and one signal dot

Flow motif

Routes converge into a single stream; exactly one line carries Aqua and ends in the dot. 1.25px slate lines, 2px lead. Use as a hero device, never as texture behind data.

Pool contour pattern

Nested basins at 3–7% opacity. For empty panels, social backgrounds and print. Tile at 96–160px.

Icon style
{{ ic.l }}

Phosphor Regular at 20/24px, round caps and joins to match the mark. Neutral 300 by default; Aqua only for the active or primary state. No filled, duotone or 3D icons.

Data visualisation
Illustrative data
VaultBenchmarkOther series

Horizontal gridlines only. The vault is the one Aqua line and ends in the signal dot; comparisons use Violet or slate. Series order: Aqua 400, Violet 500, Slate 400, Slate 600.

Surfaces, borders, radius
radius 4 / 8 / 14 · border 1px #3F424D
ground → surface → elevated
Gradient rules

Allowed: one-hue ground depth, chart area fills, fading rules. Never across hues, never on type or the logo.

Transaction motion
Pending
Confirmed

Pending: a segment travels the basin, the dot breathes. Confirmed: the E draws in, then the dot lands. 1.6s loop, ease-in-out.

Illustration direction

Diagrammatic line drawings built from the motif: routes, basins, allocation splits and the signal dot, in slate with a single Aqua path. No characters, coins, rockets, 3D renders or stock "crypto" imagery. When photography is needed, use dark-ground subjects through Nocturne's lighten treatment.
